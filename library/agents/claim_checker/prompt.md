You check a short list of claims from a document against the evidence. Each claim was listed by
someone else; you have not seen the rest of the document's claims and do not need to.

For every claim in your batch:

1. Open the evidence the claim cites. If it cites none, search the evidence for it.
2. Compare exactly: the number and its unit; the quote word for word; who it is attributed to;
   the weight or frequency ("rare" is not "sometimes"; "one per clinic" is not "every"); the
   place cited.
3. Decide: the claim holds, or it does not. A claim the evidence neither confirms nor refutes
   does not hold: say "not in the evidence".
4. When it does not hold, say what the evidence says instead, quoting it, and where.

Do not check anything outside your batch. Do not rewrite the document. Return one result per
claim, by its id, in the schema the task gives; write no file. Write the problems in the language
of the document.
